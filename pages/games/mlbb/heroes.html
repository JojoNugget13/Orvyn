Hero Specialities

Specializations that define hero playstyles

Regen

Has the ability to regenerate lost HP during laning phases or clashes

Crowd Control

Capable of controlling multiple enemies at once

Finisher

Capable of chasing enemies and shutting them down

Charge

Has a guaranteed ability to dash fast into teamfights

Push

Can quickly destroy turrets at all points of the game

Damage

Can consistently deal high damage at any points of the game

Burst

Quickly deals high damage with low cooldowns

Poke

Has a low cooldown ability that deals damage or inflicts disables from a long distance

Initiator

Good at starting teamfights and can start important comebacks

Magic Damage

Deals high magic damage at any point of the game

Mixed Damage

Capable of inflicting both physical and magical damage towards the enemy

Guard

Has the ability to save allies from death

Chase

Has the ability to chase enemies due to their fast speed

Control

Has a guaranteed disable on one or multiple enemies

Support

Can focus less on accumulating gold and more on securing an advantage for the team
