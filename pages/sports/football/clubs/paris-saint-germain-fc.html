Club History

1970: The Foundation

Paris Saint-Germain was founded on August 12, 1970, following the merger of Paris FC and Stade Saint-Germain.

Present: French Dominance

With a total of 60 trophies, PSG is the most successful club in French history, dominating Ligue 1 for the past decade.
