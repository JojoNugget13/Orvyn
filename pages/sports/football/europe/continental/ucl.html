UEFA Champions League

The ultimate stage of European club football

2024/25 Season

The 2024–25 UEFA Champions League was the 70th season of Europe's premier club football competition and the first to use the new 36 team league phase format. Paris Saint-Germain won their first Champions League title after defeating Inter Milan 5 to 0 in the final.

23/24 Season

The 2023–24 season concluded with Real Madrid securing their 15th European title. They defeated Borussia Dortmund 2–0 in the final held at Wembley Stadium, with goals from Dani Carvajal and Vinícius Júnior. This season was the last to use the 32-team group stage format.
