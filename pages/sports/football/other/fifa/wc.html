FIFA World Cup

The world's most prestigious tournament.

United States, Canada and Mexico 2026

The 2026 FIFA World Cup is the 23rd edition of the tournament and is jointly hosted by Canada, Mexico, and the United States. It is the first World Cup to feature 48 teams, with Spain winning their second title after defeating Argentina 1 to 0 in the final.

Qatar 2022

The 2022 FIFA World Cup was the 22nd edition of the tournament, hosted by Qatar from 20 November to 18 December 2022. Argentina won their third title after defeating France on penalties in the final.

23/24 Season

The 2023–24 season concluded with Real Madrid securing their 15th European title. They defeated Borussia Dortmund 2–0 in the final held at Wembley Stadium, with goals from Dani Carvajal and Vinícius Júnior. This season was the last to use the 32-team group stage format.
